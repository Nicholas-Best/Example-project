== Disposal

To protect the environment and comply with international safety standards, do not dispose of this device with household waste. Follow all local regulations regarding the disposal of electronic equipment.

=== Battery Disposal
This device contains high-capacity Lithium-Ion batteries. These must be removed from the chassis and taken to an authorized battery recycling center. Improper disposal may lead to environmental contamination or fire hazards.

=== Electronic Components
The processing core and circuit boards contain materials that can be recovered and recycled. Please use your local collection programs for the main unit.
