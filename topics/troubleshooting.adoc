== Troubleshooting

If the device fails during the initial configuration or the signal strength drops, consult the table for troubleshooting assistance.
If you need further assistance, contact {company-name} at {email}.

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Symptom | Probable cause | Resolution

| Status LED is off | Battery modules are depleted or improperly seated.
| Ensure batteries are locked into the mounting brackets and fully charged.

| Low signal range | Antennas in the transmission array are obstructed.
| Clear the top-mounted section of any metallic covers or debris. +
Move the device to an open area with no signal interfierance or metal nearby.

| Overheating | Cooling block intake is blocked.
| Inspect the base of the unit and clear airflow obstructions.
|===
