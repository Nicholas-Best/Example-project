=== Inspecting the {product-name}

include::../../snippets/electrical.adoc[]
:warning-body: Damaged lithium-ion batteries can explode! +
Make sure the batteries do have any physical damage, such as dents or swollen areas.

Before assembling the device, you must make sure that all of the components are undamaged by inspecting the device and its accessories.

*To inspect the {product-name}, do the following:*

* Visually inspect the RF antennas for any physical damage, such as a bent antenna o broken connection.
* Visually inspect the {product-name} chasis for any damage that could affect the internal circuitry.
* Visually inspecy the batteries for any damage.
