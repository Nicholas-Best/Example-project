:note-body: The chasis connections are preconnected. You do not have to modify the chasis connections.

The {product-name} must be installed on a clean surface and free of debris. Before power the device, make sure no electronic device that could be damaged is in range.

*To assemble the {product-name}, do the following:*

. Open the device's front chasis.
. Using the marking on the chasis and circuit board, place the main circuit board inside the device.
The markings must align to form a single straight line.
. Using four 5 mm phillips screws, secure the circuit board to the chasis.
. On the top of the device, install the radio frequency antennas by using the antenna screw.
. Connect the main power connection from the circuit board to the chasis.
For more infomation, refer to <<Main circuit board PCBU>>

. Place the lithium-ion batteries inside the bottom battery section.
. Push the batteries until you hear a snap.
. Power the device and run functionality tests.
. Using four 7 mm phillips screws, install the chasis front cover.
