=== Mounting the {product-name} on a wall

include::../../snippets/pinch-point.adoc[]

You can install the {product-name} on a wall using the chasis wall mount. You can remove the device from the wall mount by pressing the locking mechanism on the side of the wall mount.

*To install the {product-name} on a wall, do the following:*

. Install the wall mount on the desired surface.
. Hold the {product-name} on both sides, making sure no object is between the central mounting and the wall mount.
. Slowly slide the {product-name} over the wall mount until you hear a snap.

*To remove the {product-name} from a wall mount, do the following:*

. Press the lock switch on both side of the wall mount.
The locking mechanism snaps.
. Slide the {product-name} upwards.
